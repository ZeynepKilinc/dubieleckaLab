Patrycja’s Full Bio

Dr. Dubielecka is an Associate Professor of Medicine at Brown University and Director of Translational Hematology at Rhode Island Hospital. Her research focuses on blood cancer-initiating stem cells and their role in the development, progression, and relapse of myeloproliferative neoplasms, with the goal of identifying new therapeutic strategies.
