<!-- excerpt start -->Alissa Oakes successfully defended her PhD thesis, “The Complement Cascade in Myeloproliferative Neoplasms,” on June 6, 2024.<!-- excerpt end -->

As a member of Dr. Patrycja Dubielecka-Szczerba’s laboratory at Brown University, Alissa studied blood cancer-initiating stem cells and their roles in the development, progression, and relapse of myeloproliferative neoplasms. Her work contributed to the lab’s translational goal of identifying ways to target neoplasm-initiating stem cells.

Following her successful defense, Alissa celebrated in Sidney Frank Hall with her advisor, lab mates, family, and members of the Brown community. Her colleagues marked the occasion with a playful lab-themed refreshment: cranberry and pineapple juice served in blood-donor bags. Alissa’s daughter attended both the defense and celebration, and family members traveled from across the United States to share the milestone.

Congratulations, Dr. Oakes!
