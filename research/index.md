# Research

Our research investigates how aging, chronic inflammation, and the bone marrow microenvironment shape blood cancer development, progression, and relapse.

<div class="research-area-grid home-research-grid">
  <article class="home-research-card">
    <img src="{{ '/images/research/lab-tubes.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Laboratory glass tubes filled with colored solutions">
    <h3>Basic Science</h3>
    <p>We investigate how aging and chronic inflammation alter hematopoietic stem cells and the bone marrow niche. Our work explores the cellular interactions and signaling mechanisms that support malignant transformation and blood cancer progression.</p>
    <a href="{{ '/discoveries/' | relative_url }}">Explore our discoveries</a>
  </article>
  <article class="home-research-card research-area-schematic">
    <img src="{{ '/images/research/data-screens.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Computer monitors displaying charts and data">
    <h3>Translational Research</h3>
    <p>We connect discoveries in experimental models with questions relevant to human disease. Using cellular and molecular profiling, we investigate vulnerabilities in disease-initiating stem cells to inform new therapeutic strategies.</p>
    <a href="#technologies-and-approaches">Our approaches</a>
  </article>
  <article class="home-research-card">
    <img src="{{ '/images/research/stethoscope.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Stethoscope on a light surface">
    <h3>Clinical Research</h3>
    <p>We collaborate with clinicians to investigate how disease biology relates to patient outcomes. Our interests include biomarkers, immune alterations, and clinical factors associated with the course of hematologic malignancies.</p>
    <a href="{{ '/research/' | relative_url }}">Read our publications</a>
  </article>
</div>
