## Postdoctoral Researchers

For prospective postdoctoral researchers, we suggest a background in hematology, stem cell biology, immunology, cancer biology, computational biology, or a related field. Experience with one or more relevant approaches—such as flow cytometry, mass cytometry, spatial transcriptomics, single-cell profiling, bone marrow tissue analysis, cellular profiling, or molecular interaction studies—would be valuable. We also welcome researchers whose complementary expertise could bring new perspectives to the lab.

Interested candidates can contact [Dr. Patrycja Dubielecka directly](mailto:Patrycja_Dubielecka-Szczerba@brown.edu) to discuss their research interests and potential opportunities, or complete the shared interest form below once it becomes available.

## Graduate Students

Our lab brings together students from MCB, Pathobiology, CCMB, and Biotechnology, reflecting the broad and interdisciplinary nature of our research. Admitted graduate students from these or other departments who are interested in a rotation or graduate research in the Dubielecka Lab are welcome to fill out the shared interest form below. Please tell us about your program, research interests, and what you hope to explore in the lab.

## Undergraduate Students

Enthusiastic undergraduate students who would like to become involved in the lab are encouraged to fill out the shared interest form below. We welcome students who are eager to learn, ask questions, and contribute to a collaborative research environment. Please share your interests, any relevant coursework or research experience, and your anticipated availability.
