Janet O. Joseph is a PhD student in the Pathobiology Graduate Program at Brown University, working under Dr. Laura Stroud and Patrycja Dubielecka. Her research focuses on chemical and biological stressors and transcriptomic markers of the placenta, including inflammatory and neuroinflammatory markers important for immunological responses during pregnancy.
