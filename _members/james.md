James He is an MD-PhD student in the Molecular Biology, Cellular Biology, and Biochemistry Graduate Program (MCBGP) in the Dubielecka Lab at Brown University. His research focuses on profiling dysfunctional protein signaling networks that underlie the progression of myeloproliferative neoplasms (MPNs), a class of hematologic cancers arising in the bone marrow. Using in vitro and in vivo model systems, he is employing proximity proteomics approaches to derive the pathogenic interactomes of MPN oncoproteins. He is dedicated to the bench-to-bedside pathway and intends to translate his research to actionable components of patient care. Outside of the lab, James spends his time exploring the outdoors, trying new foods, playing sports, and enjoying quality time with friends and family.
