Alissa Oakes, PhD is an alumna of the Dubielecka Lab at Brown University. Her doctoral research examined blood cancer-initiating stem cells and the complement cascade in myeloproliferative neoplasms. She successfully defended her thesis, “The Complement Cascade in Myeloproliferative Neoplasms,” on June 6, 2024.

[Read Brown University’s announcement](https://tsgp.brown.edu/news/2024-08-29/congratulations-dr-oakes).
